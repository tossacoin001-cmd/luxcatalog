Drop paid Clerk features: custom roles and satellite domains cost $110+/mo in production

Checked Clerk's actual pricing: going to production is free, but the two
features this session added, custom org roles and satellite domains, both
require Clerk's B2B add-on ($100/mo, plus $10/mo per satellite). Reworked
around the free tier instead of migrating off Clerk entirely.

Partner status ("org:vendor") is no longer a real Clerk role. It's now
Clerk's free org:member role plus a publicMetadata.partnerType flag set at
invite time, resolved back into the same 'org:vendor' value everywhere else
in the app via a single resolveRole() helper, so none of the downstream
admin/vendor logic needed to change.

Admin is back to being a role-gated section of the main site instead of a
separate satellite-domain deployment, removes the cross-domain session
complexity (and its cost) entirely. proxy.ts and the ClerkProvider are back
to plain single-domain config.
