# Team rewards: where the money comes from, and how trust is protected

Written 2026-10-24, after the Founder's answers on `docs/TEAM-PROGRAM.md`. Decided: **no withdrawable tokens (D7 yes)**; everyone sees their own rank, the top five and "most improved", only the Founder sees everyone (D10 yes). The rates below are proposals until the Founder confirms D8.

## 1. The rule that protects everyone

**A team reward is paid from Lux Catalog's own fee, after it has been received, and never from the listing price.**

- Partners are paid exactly what their agreement says. A team reward never reduces a partner payout.
- Clients never pay a cent more because a team member brought them.
- If Lux Catalog earns nothing on a booking (cancelled, refunded, test, fraud), nobody on the team earns on it either.
- The base is the **net fee**: our commission minus the payment-processing cost and any refund. That is the real profit on that booking, so a reward can never be more than we kept.

## 2. What Lux Catalog already charges (the fee the reward comes from)

From `DEFAULT_COMMISSION` in the code (real values live in the commission rules and can be set per partner):

| Luxury shortlet, 3 nights at ₦300,000 | 15% | ₦900,000 | ₦135,000 | ₦13,500 |
| Car hire, 2 days at ₦350,000 | 18% | ₦700,000 | ₦126,000 | ₦12,600 |
| Boat cruise | 13% | ₦1,000,000 | ₦130,000 | ₦13,000 |
| Private chef session | 20% | ₦45,000 | ₦9,000 | ₦900 |
| Home to rent, one year | 5% (capped) | ₦25,000,000 | ₦1,250,000 | ₦125,000 |
| Home to buy | 3% | ₦900,000,000 | ₦27,000,000 | ₦2,700,000 |

Reading it honestly:

- Stays, hire, cruises and services give **small, frequent, safe** amounts. A percentage of the fee works well there.
- Rentals and sales give **very large, rare** amounts. A flat percentage would hand one lucky referral a huge sum and make the platform depend on a few deals. **Do not use a percentage there.** Use a fixed finder's fee for each deal, written down before the deal and approved by the Founder.

## 3. Property sales and rentals need legal advice first

- Lagos regulates estate agency by law. The sources quote an agency fee of 10% of annual rent, report a bill to cut it to 5% (its status was not confirmed), and say registration of agents is required or proposed. Names of the regulator differ between sources.
- Paying team members a commission on a property transaction could make them, or Lux Catalog, an unregistered "agent" in the eyes of the regulator.
- **Recommendation:** start on **stays, hire, cruises and services only.** For sales and rentals a team member hands the lead to a registered partner or to the Founder, and any fee is agreed with a lawyer first.

## 4. How other marketplaces protect trust in the same situation (patterns, not rules)

- **A share of the platform's own commission, never of the price.** Booking.com's affiliate programme pays partners a share of Booking.com's commission (reported 25% to 40%, rising with monthly volume) and nothing on cancelled or no-show stays.
- **Pay after the event, not at sign-up.** Airbnb pays after the referred host's booking is completed; Uber after the referred driver finishes the trips.
- **A hold period and a reversal rule, stated in advance.** Programmes hold a commission until the refund window has passed (30 to 60 days in software). For us the natural moment is the same one the partner is paid. A commission still pending when a refund arrives is cancelled. One already paid is taken from the next payment, not demanded back (fraud excepted). Every reversal stays visible with its reason.
- **Rates change only for the future.** Our partner agreement already says this; the team terms say the same.

Several of the sources are vendor blogs or conflict with each other, so these are patterns to follow, not numbers to copy.

## 5. The Team Terms (one short page every member sees, and agrees to)

1. A reward is a share of Lux Catalog's net fee on a booking you brought, after the guest's payment has cleared and the partner has been paid.
2. You see every line in your dashboard: the booking, our net fee, your share, its status and, if cancelled, why.
3. Rates change only for bookings made after the change, with notice.
4. A cancelled or refunded booking earns nothing. A reward already paid for a booking that is later refunded is taken from your next reward, not asked back from you. Fraud ends the arrangement and forfeits unpaid rewards.
5. Your own account, your family's, and test bookings earn nothing.
6. Nothing is promised in advance. A month with no fee brings no reward.
7. You are paid to your verified bank account, after any tax the law requires.
8. If you disagree with a line, you raise it and we answer within 7 days with the booking record.

## 6. Reasons to complete daily tasks while there is no revenue

Cash cannot be the daily motivator today, and the research agrees it should not be the only one. What the evidence supports, and what costs nothing:

- **Personal feedback each week**, and **a say in how a share of future money is used.** A field study found both raised output where only intrinsic motivation was available.
- **Recognition that is specific and visible to the team.** Volunteer programmes lost people when appreciation was removed.
- **Learning and connection:** short training, somewhere to ask questions, a named mentor, a reference letter from the Founder.
- **Real progression:** levels that unlock real things: first pick of new enquiries, a higher referral rate later, the best listings and viewings.
- **A Founding Team status** for the first members: a locked-in referral rate for their first 12 months of earning, and first call on paid roles when revenue allows. It costs nothing until money exists, and says so honestly.
- **A Team Fund vote (later):** when a monthly pool exists, a small slice is spent the way the team votes.

The daily table gives a personal score and streak. The scoreboard shows own rank, the top five and most improved (D10).

## 7. Tax, explained plainly (what D11 means)

When a business pays someone a commission, Nigerian tax rules can require the business to **hold back a small percentage and pay it to the tax office on the person's behalf.** This is called withholding tax. The person receives a credit note and the amount counts toward their own income tax, so it is not a fine on them.

- The rate quoted for commission to a person who is not a company is **5%** (10% in some cases). The new tax act took effect in January 2026, so a professional must confirm today's figure.
- **The risk is ours, not theirs:** the sources report a penalty of 40% of the amount if a business should have held it back and did not.
- **What we do now, without waiting:** the system records for every payment the full amount, the tax held back and the net paid. A tax ID (TIN) field is added to the team profile. The Team Terms say payments are made after any tax the law requires.
- **What the Founder does, once, before the first payout:** ask an accountant three questions. (1) What rate applies to commission paid to individuals? (2) Must we issue credit notes, and how? (3) Does the business need to be a registered company with a tax ID to pay commissions? Then tell me the answers and I set the rate in one place.

## 8. Decisions

- D7 no tokens: **yes (Founder, 2026-10-24).**
- D8 rates (proposed, only for stays, hire, cruises and services): 10% of net fee for a client or partner you brought (12 months); 15% for an enquiry you personally close (one booking); a fixed bonus for a teammate who completes a first paid booking. Rentals and sales: no percentage, a fixed fee agreed per deal with legal advice.
- D9 monthly pool: only once there is revenue.
- D10 scoreboard: **yes (Founder, 2026-10-24).**
- D11 tax: explained above; one accountant visit before the first payout.
